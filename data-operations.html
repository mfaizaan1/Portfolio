Data Infrastructure Operation

Data Visualization Project

Project Details

This Data Infrastructure Operation Dashboard is built for sellers who need to present a realistic AI cloud infrastructure operations story with clarity and business value. It highlights Northstar Compute, a fictional but realistic mid-sized cloud infrastructure provider that operates regional data centers and sells computing capacity to AI startups, SaaS companies, enterprise customers, and research organizations.

Northstar customers purchase GPU compute hours, CPU compute hours, storage, and network egress. As AI demand accelerates growth, management needs to understand whether revenue is rising profitably or whether infrastructure costs and capacity constraints are growing even faster.

Business Objective

Monitor whether Northstar Compute is growing profitably without running out of infrastructure capacity.

Tools Used

Power BI and Power Query.

DAX Measures

This project uses DAX measures to calculate business metrics including Capacity Utilization %, Cost per Compute Hour, Gross Margin %, Average Customer Satisfaction, Gross Profit, Revenue, Revenue per Compute Hour, SLA Reliability %, Total Cost, and Total Orders.

Capacity Utilization % = DIVIDE(SUM(Usage_Fact[Usage_Hours]), SUM(Usage_Fact[Capacity_Available_Hours]))

Average Customer Satisfaction = AVERAGE(Usage_Fact[Customer_Satisfaction])

Cost per Compute Hour =
DIVIDE(
    [Total Cost],
    SUM('Usage_Fact'[Usage_Hours])
)

Gross Margin % = DIVIDE([Gross Profit], [Revenue])

Gross Profit = SUM(Usage_Fact[Revenue_USD]) - (SUM(Usage_Fact[Cloud_Provider_Cost_USD]) + SUM(Usage_Fact[Energy_Cost_USD]) + SUM(Usage_Fact[Infrastructure_Cost_USD]) + SUM(Usage_Fact[Support_Cost_USD]) + SUM(Usage_Fact[SLA_Credits_USD]))

Revenue = SUM(Usage_Fact[Revenue_USD])

Revenue per Compute Hour = DIVIDE([Revenue], SUM(Usage_Fact[Usage_Hours]))

SLA Reliability % =
1 -
DIVIDE(
    SUM('Usage_Fact'[Outage_Hours]),
    COUNTROWS('Usage_Fact') * 720
)

Total Cost =
SUM('Usage_Fact'[Infrastructure_Cost_USD])
+
SUM('Usage_Fact'[Energy_Cost_USD])
+
SUM('Usage_Fact'[Cloud_Provider_Cost_USD])
+
SUM('Usage_Fact'[Support_Cost_USD])
+
SUM('Usage_Fact'[SLA_Credits_USD])

Total Orders = SUM(Usage_Fact[Orders])
